What a first conversation with a prospective EXE Capital Wealth Management client is designed to establish — before any structuring recommendation is made.

What This Conversation Covers
📍

Segment

Is the family Resident, Non-Resident, or Transnational? This determines which parts of the Toolkit and Reference Library are most relevant from the outset.

📜

Current Succession Position

Is a DIFC Will already registered? Is there an existing trust, foundation, or PPLI structure anywhere in the family's holdings? This sets the baseline for the Forced-Heirship Exposure tool.

🏦

Scope of Advisory Need

Private client advisory only, or interest in RWA advisory and/or private capital advisory alongside it — and whether custody/settlement needs point toward EXE Bank or a correspondent custodian.

What Happens Next
  1. Initial conversation (30–45 minutes) to establish segment, jurisdictional footprint, and current succession documentation.
  2. EXE Capital Wealth Management runs the family's profile through the Segmentation & Readiness tool and the Forced-Heirship Exposure simulator (Toolkit tabs 2 and 3) to produce an illustrative structuring priority.
  3. A short written structuring roadmap is prepared, referencing the specific Reference Library appendices relevant to that family (DIFC Will, Foundation vs. Trust, Golden Visa, cross-border PPLI, as applicable).
  4. Introduction to the relevant advisor bench — DIFC/ADGM counsel, and Malcolm Dermit for cross-border PPLI where relevant — to begin implementation.
