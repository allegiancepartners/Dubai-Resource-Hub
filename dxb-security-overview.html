How Allegiance Partners uses Claude (Anthropic) for drafting and analysis on EXE Capital's behalf, and how that fits UAE data-protection expectations.

  1. Data handling. Client and prospect information used in Claude-assisted drafting and analysis is handled under Anthropic's standard data-handling terms; conversation content is not used to train Anthropic's models.
  2. Certifications. Anthropic maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications, consistent with the standards a DFSA-regulated wealth manager would expect of a technology vendor.
  3. UAE & DIFC data protection. For a DIFC-domiciled business, the relevant frameworks are the DIFC Data Protection Law (DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020) and, more broadly, the UAE's Federal Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) — the direct counterparts to the GDPR/CCPA references in Allegiance's US security overview. Confirm current data-residency and cross-border transfer requirements under both frameworks before handling UAE client data through any AI tool.
  4. Fiduciary obligations unaffected. Use of Claude for drafting, research, or scenario modeling does not change EXE Capital Wealth Management's fiduciary and regulatory obligations to its clients — every output is reviewed by a qualified professional before it reaches a client.
This overview is illustrative and general. DIFC/ADGM-qualified counsel and EXE Capital's own compliance function should confirm current data-protection and outsourcing requirements before any client data is processed through Claude or any other AI tool in production.
