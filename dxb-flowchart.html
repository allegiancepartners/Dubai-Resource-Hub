How a client moves from first conversation to an ongoing EXE Capital Wealth Management advisory relationship, and where the Toolkit and Reference Library plug into that process.

Process Diagram
Stage Detail
  1. Prospective Client — introduced via EXE Capital's local partner network (most often Shams/DWTC), or directly to Allegiance/EXE Capital Wealth Management.
  2. Segmentation — the Discovery Conversation establishes whether the family is Resident, Non-Resident, or Transnational, which determines the weighting of everything downstream.
  3. Structuring Engine — DIFC Will registration, and/or a DIFC or ADGM Foundation or Trust, sized to the family's forced-heirship exposure (Toolkit, tabs 1–3).
  4. Golden Visa & Residency — evaluated in parallel where relevant, particularly for Non-Resident families considering relocation.
  5. Cross-Border Coordination — for Non-Resident and Transnational families, aligning the UAE structure with home-jurisdiction estate/tax counsel, and evaluating a cross-border PPLI wrapper with Malcolm Dermit where appropriate.

All five stages are documented, and can be revisited, inside the Succession & Structuring Toolkit — the client-facing deliverable this process produces.
