Regulatory Map
🏛️

DIFC / DFSA

Dubai's original financial free zone, English common law, independent courts. The natural home for onshore wealth management — recommended domicile for EXE Capital Wealth Management. Licensing scales from light-touch Single Family Office registration to a full discretionary asset/wealth manager license.

🏛️

ADGM / FSRA

Abu Dhabi's rival financial free zone — also English common law, faster recent AUM/registration growth. Referenced here principally for its Foundations and Trusts regimes (Reference Library, Appendix C), which are frequently used alongside a DIFC-domiciled advisory relationship.

⛓️

VARA

Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority — governs EXE Bank's settlement, custody, and token-economy infrastructure. VARA's regulations explicitly exclude DIFC from their scope, which is why EXE Bank sits outside the wealth-management entity rather than inside it.

Professional Associations
🌐

STEP (UAE Chapter)

The Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners has an active UAE branch — the most directly transferable credentialing body from Allegiance's US bench, given its global, cross-border focus.

⚖️

DIFC Courts & DIFC Wills Service Registry

Not a membership association, but the operational counterpart to STEP for this market: the registry through which a will electing non-Sharia succession is filed and recognized (Reference Library, Appendix A).

📋

US associations — not directly transferable

NAEPC, the Family Office Exchange, the Purposeful Planning Institute, the Council on Foundations, and ACGA are US-centric (largely tied to the 501(c)(3)/DAF ecosystem) and have no direct UAE equivalent. Retained here only as a note, not as referral sources for this market.

Trusted Advisor Bench

Under the operational philosophy behind this build, EXE Capital Wealth Management keeps a single in-house Wealth Strategist rather than a full bench of in-house Estate, Trust & Tax specialists — that Strategist manages a small set of external strategic partnerships instead, on a fixed-to-floating cost basis, with preferential terms negotiated for EXE Capital's clients.

Specialist international law firm with a Dubai/DIFC presence. Anchor legal partner for DIFC Will registration, Foundation/Trust drafting, DFSA licensing support, and structuring Wealth Advisor compensation (Operational Philosophy, §8.2).

EXE Capital's Local Partners (Dubai)
🤝

Burhan

Credit risk. Brings the risk-management infrastructure a licensed wealth manager needs, and the closest internal link to EXE Bank's balance-sheet functions.

🏛️

Khaldun

Strategic relationships — adviser to HH Sheikh Mansour. Brings high-level credibility and access that would take years for a new entrant to build independently.

📈

Business development at Dubai World Trade Center. Flagged as the single most important client-acquisition asset — attracting clients, not building infrastructure, is the hardest part of starting a new wealth management operation in this market.
